Phil spent 21 years in the Royal Navy Police, specialising in intelligence and project management. Since leaving the armed forces, he has pursued a career in the environmental sector. Phil’s passions lie in spending time in the great outdoors and travelling to remote locations around the world. Having canoed rivers and surfed in countries across the globe, Phil is a firm believer in the significant benefits spending time outdoors has upon mental health. What’s more, he is an advocate for the benefits that time spent in the natural environment can bring to society as well. However, he has also seen and personally experienced the impact of pollution and climate change, and so is keen to help improve rivers, waterways and the environment to help restore natural habitats and to benefit future generations.
