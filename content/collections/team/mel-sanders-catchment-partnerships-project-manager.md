Having recently left her role as River Recovery Manager at the Knepp Wildland Foundation leading the Adur Landscape Recovery project, Mel brings a wealth of experience and knowledge to her role as Catchment Partnerships Project Manager. Focusing on developing a programme of works for the Arun and Western Stream catchment, Mel will be working closely with the catchment partnership and Southern Water. Growing up on the upper reaches of the River Thames and seeing/hearing the curlews displaying, Mel has always had a deep love for all things rivers, with some of her best childhood memories involving playing and paddling in the water cress beds and pond dipping! Mel will now be taking her learnings and successes from the Adur project and applying them to the Arun to accelerate nature recovery across the catchment.
